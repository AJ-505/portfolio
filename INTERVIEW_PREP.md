## D. Emerging tech & financial services (33–45)

**33. What emerging technologies will most impact financial services?**
Agentic AI (AI that takes actions, not just answers), generative AI, cloud, open banking/APIs, and blockchain/digital assets. The biggest near-term shift is agentic AI — already around 70% of banks are exploring or using it for things like fraud detection, customer service, and operations.

**34. What is agentic AI and why does it matter to a bank?**
It's AI that can plan and execute multi-step tasks autonomously, not just generate text. For a bank that means automating real workflows — onboarding, compliance checks, fraud triage — with a human overseeing rather than doing every step. It moves AI from "assistant" to "operator," which is a step-change in efficiency.

**35. What are the risks of AI in financial services?**
Bias in decisions (lending), explainability (regulators need to know why), data privacy, hallucination, and model governance. This is exactly why EY's role matters — adoption has to be paired with risk and assurance. The opportunity and the risk are two sides of the same engagement.

**36. How can EY help clients adopt these technologies?**
Three ways: strategy (where AI actually creates value), implementation (building it), and assurance/risk (doing it safely and compliantly). EY is rare in covering all three, which is why clients trust it for high-stakes tech adoption.

**37. What's happening in Nigerian fintech?**
It's one of Africa's biggest markets — 430+ fintechs and roughly $2B in funding raised in recent years, with players like Flutterwave, Paystack, and OPay. Drivers are a young, mobile-first population, high financial-exclusion to solve, and supportive rails. Huge opportunity, and huge need for the governance and trust EY provides.

**38. What opportunities does this create for EY Nigeria?**
As fintechs scale and traditional banks digitise, they need help with AI adoption, cloud migration, cybersecurity, regulatory compliance, and risk. EY can be the trusted advisor that lets them move fast without breaking trust — which in finance is everything.

**39. How is AI changing the accounting/audit side, not just tech?**
AI automates routine data work — reconciliation, anomaly detection across full datasets instead of samples — so audit becomes more continuous and insight-driven. It raises quality and frees people for judgement work. EY is already building this into how it delivers.

**40. What's the difference between generative and agentic AI?**
Generative AI produces content — text, code, images — in response to a prompt. Agentic AI uses that capability inside a loop to pursue a goal: it plans, calls tools, acts, checks the result, and adapts. Generative answers; agentic does.

**41. Open banking — what is it and why does it matter here?**
It's standardised APIs that let customers securely share their financial data between providers. It enables better products — lending, budgeting, payments — and more competition. In Nigeria, with a regulatory framework emerging, it could meaningfully expand financial inclusion.

**42. What about blockchain / digital assets in finance?**
Beyond crypto, the real value is in settlement, cross-border payments, and transparent record-keeping. Adoption is cautious because of regulation, but cross-border payments — a real pain point in Africa — are a credible near-term use case.

**43. What technology trend are you personally most excited about?**
Agentic AI — because it changes what software can do, from answering to acting. The interesting and hard part is governance: how you let it act autonomously while staying safe and compliant. That's precisely the problem financial services has to solve, and where EY adds value.

**44. How would you advise a cautious bank that's nervous about AI?**
Start where the risk is contained and the value is clear — internal efficiency or fraud detection, not customer-facing lending decisions on day one. Build governance alongside, keep a human in the loop, prove value on a small scope, then scale. Trust is earned incrementally.

**45. What's a concrete way AI saves a financial institution money?**
Fraud detection is the clearest: AI catches patterns across millions of transactions in real time that rules-based systems and humans miss — reducing losses and false positives at once. Customer service automation and document processing are close behind.
